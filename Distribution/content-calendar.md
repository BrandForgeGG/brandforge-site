## Posting rules reference

- First line of every announcement: "Describe it. Humans build it."
- Never use hype adjectives: amazing, incredible, game-changing, revolutionary
- Always include a CTA
- One idea per post
- Short lines. No paragraph longer than 4 sentences
- No em dashes, no passive voice, no AI jargon
- Match the tone: sharp, warm, human
