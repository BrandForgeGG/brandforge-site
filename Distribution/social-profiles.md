## Content posting guidelines

### Frequency

- Discord: 2-3 posts per week in #announcements, daily moderation in #general
- Telegram channel: 3 posts per week
- Telegram group: Daily activity, respond within 4 hours

### Rules

- One idea per post. Short lines.
- Lead with the outcome, not the technology.
- Always include a next step (try it, join Discord, apply).
- Never invent metrics, user counts, or testimonials.
- No hype adjectives. Do not say "amazing," "incredible," "game-changing."
- Match the tone: sharp, warm, human. Not corporate, not casual.
- Post at these times: Tuesday 9am, Thursday 1pm, Sunday 6pm (UTC).
- Tagline or positioning statement must appear in the first line of every announcement post.
