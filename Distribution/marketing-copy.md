## Product description

### Short (1 sentence)

BrandForge turns a message into a shipped project with AI structure, vetted humans, and escrow protection.

### Medium (3-5 sentences)

BrandForge is a chat-first platform for building digital products. You describe what you want to build, AI structures it into a clear plan, and a vetted human team designs, builds and ships it. Your budget sits in escrow until you approve the delivered work. No dashboards. No ticket queues. No freelance platforms. Just one conversation that goes from idea to shipped product.

### Long (paragraph form)

BrandForge exists because building software should not require browsing freelance platforms, writing detailed briefs, and negotiating contracts with strangers. You open a chat, describe what you want to build, and the system takes it from there. AI structures your description into a project brief with clear scope, milestones, and deliverables. A vetted human team matched to your skills and budget takes over from there — designing, building, and shipping the real product. Your money sits in escrow the entire time. You approve the work before paying. There is no dashboard, no status tracker, no ticket queue. Everything happens in the conversation. If you have a Google account, you can sign in right now and post your first project. That is the whole product.

## Press release draft

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**BrandForge launches: one conversation replaces the freelance hiring process**

BrandForge, a chat-first project platform, is live at brandforge.gg. The product replaces traditional freelance hiring tools with a single conversation: a user describes what they want to build, AI structures the request into a project brief, and a vetted human team designs, builds and ships the result — with escrow protecting the client's budget.

No dashboards. No ticket queues. No job boards. The entire product lives inside a chat interface.

"Building software should not require browsing profiles, writing detailed briefs, and negotiating with strangers," says the BrandForge team. "You describe what you want. We handle the rest."

Key features include:

- Chat-first project workflow with no separate dashboards or status trackers
- AI-powered project structuring from a single description
- Vetted human teams matched by skill and project type
- Escrow-protected payments with approval-based fund release
- Open authentication with any Google account, no waiting list

BrandForge is available now at brandforge.gg. The community server is at https://discord.gg/GSKHXkUY85.

**About BrandForge**

BrandForge is a chat-first platform where conversations become projects. The company operates with two departments — development and distribution — that work on the same projects they ship for customers.
