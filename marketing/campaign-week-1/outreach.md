# Week 1 outreach: DMs and emails that start conversations

Send 10 a day. Personalise the first line. Never paste a link in the first message to someone who has not replied before.

## 1. Existing clients and reviewers (fastest money)
These people already trust you. Ask for a monthly plan before you ask anyone else.

> Hi {name}, thanks again for your kind words about working with us. We have just opened monthly plans: a person on our team plus AI delivering {their thing: content / ads / site upkeep} every month for a fixed price (from €490). You would keep the same people. Want me to send what that would look like for you? If you agree, I will keep your price fixed for the first six months. *(Your call: only offer the fixed price if you will honour it.)*

Also ask: "Can we use your name next to your review?"

## 2. Small businesses and creators (the plans)
> Hi {name}, I saw {specific thing they posted}. We run a small team that, with AI doing the first drafts, handles a month of content for you: carousels, captions, a calendar, and a weekly note on what worked. It starts at €490 and you only pay once we agree what you get. Want a quick look at what your first month would be?

## 3. Higher-value clients (bigger projects, from €5,000)
> Hi {name}, we are the BrandForge team: we scope and build products, platforms and bots with fixed-price proposals and milestone payments held in escrow, by card or crypto. If you have something on the roadmap that needs a named lead and a small team, I can send a one-page proposal after a 15-minute call. Page: {ENTERPRISE LINK}

## 4. Investors and angels
> Hi {name}, I am building BrandForge: AI makes the first draft and people finish it, with contracts and escrow underneath. It is early, live, and shipping weekly: a chat workspace, an open marketplace (Trade), and a team that delivers for clients. We publish only numbers we can show and share the rest in a data room. Would a 20-minute call this week be useful? {INVESTORS LINK}

## 5. Developers and designers
> Hi {name}, we pay developers and designers per milestone through escrow, on briefs that are already scoped. Small team, direct chat with the lead. If that sounds like work you would take, here is how it works: {DEVELOPERS LINK}

## 6. Communities and partners (give first)
> Hi {admin}, I run BrandForge. I would like to offer your members a free walkthrough of the chat that makes carousels and listings from one sentence. Would a short post or a live session be welcome? I will answer every question.

## Directories and launches (use the 53 targets in Admin > Campaigns)
- **Order:** the easiest approvals first (AI directories, startup lists), then Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Hacker News "Show HN".
- **Show HN title idea:** "Show HN: BrandForge, a chat that makes content and hires a human to finish it".
- **Product Hunt:** launch on a weekday with a hunter if you can; prepare 4 screenshots (chat, carousel in chat, Trade, pricing), one 30s video and a first comment that tells the story.
- **Honest limits to state:** publishing to some platforms is still waiting on platform approval.

## Objection handling
- **"I can do this with ChatGPT."** → "You can draft with it. We add a person who finishes and publishes it every month, so you do not have to."
- **"€490 is a lot."** → "It replaces a month of someone's time and you only pay once we agree the scope. Start with one month."
- **"What if I do not like it?"** → "Cancel any month. Bigger projects pay per milestone, held in escrow."
- **"Who is on the team?"** → "A named lead plus a designer and developers. Open the live projects: {WORK LINK}."
