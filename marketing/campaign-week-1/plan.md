## Daily routine (about 90 minutes of your own time)
1. **Morning (15 min):** post the day's X post 1 and the LinkedIn post. Confirm the Discord and Telegram posts went out.
2. **Midday (30 min):** answer every comment, reply and DM. Post X post 2. Send 10 outreach messages from `outreach.md`.
3. **Afternoon (15 min):** post the short video (script in `copy.md`) to TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
4. **Evening (30 min):** post X post 3, reply again, write down in the tracker: visits, chats started, `plan_interest` and `plan_requested` events, replies, calls booked.

- **Admin > Funnel:** chats started, plan interest, plan requests (new this week).
